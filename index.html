1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Bakchal (बाघचाल), the premier digital edition of Nepal's traditional 2-player strategic board game (Tigers vs Goats). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play Bakchal on Android devices via Google Play.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • Guest Mode (Default): You can enjoy the full game completely offline (Player vs AI and Local Pass & Play) without creating an account or providing any personal information.
  • User Account & Registration (Optional): If you choose to register and log in, we collect your chosen username, email address, password hash, and gameplay statistics (coins, win/loss history, Himalayan Tier rating) to synchronize your progress and support competitive leaderboards.
  • Local Pass & Play Mode: When two players play on the same physical device, all moves run 100% locally on the device with zero external data transmission.

3. Virtual Currency & In-Game Items Policy

Virtual Coins Policy:

All coins and tokens within Bakchal are strictly virtual gameplay tokens for in-game matches, achievements, and aesthetic rewards. They hold zero real-world cash or monetary value, cannot be redeemed for fiat currency, and do not constitute real-money gambling.

4. Advertising & Family Safety

Bakchal adheres strictly to Google Play's Families Policy and Developer Program Policies regarding advertising. We only use certified family-safe ad networks, with user-initiated rewarded ads and zero intrusive popups during active gameplay.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Bakchal is an ancient mathematical and strategic cultural heritage game appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may request full deletion of your registered account, game ratings, and transaction logs at any time by contacting our support team or via account settings inside the app.

7. Terms of Service
